# Learnings from the frozen-data replay

Changing the uncertainty unit from family to order changes which untrained comparisons are conclusive. Bootstrapping the modal-layer estimate answers a different question from estimating uncertainty for the complete layer-selection procedure. The strict fine-split subset and broad within-order subset have different denominators. A global maximum over layers and the result for a particular layer have different null distributions.
