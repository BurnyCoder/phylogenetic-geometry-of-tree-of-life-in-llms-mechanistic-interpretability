# Learnings from the intervention experiment

The historical swap rate counts post-edit donor predictions. The stored marginals bound newly acquired donor answers, but the per-pair random and probe changes are unavailable. The literal C-swap test is unevaluable. C-abl does not clear its random-subspace comparison. The class-only results are descriptive and the pairwise questions reuse species. Storing the trial identifiers and both predictions for each edit and comparator makes the targeted transitions directly countable. Expanded random draws were chosen after inspecting the first run.
