# Summary: phylogenetic geometry of the tree of life in an LLM

*Libor "Burny" Burian.*

**Question.** Does a text LLM (Qwen3.5-2B) represent animal species so that their geometry follows the phylogenetic tree? If so, what shape does that geometry have? The template is Goodfire's analysis of Evo 2 ([phylogeny manifold](https://www.goodfire.com/research/phylogeny-manifold)) and its neural-geometry studies ([index](https://www.goodfire.com/research/neural-geometry)).

**Design.**
- **Data.** 400 species frozen before any activation was seen; 8 chat-format prompts each; residual stream at the last name token, all layers.
- **Primary metric.** Rooted-triplet accuracy restricted to triplets inside one coarse group whose names share no head noun (TA_within). Global correlations would be inflated by folk categories, so they cannot serve as the primary test.
- **Controls.** An architecture-matched random-init model, Pythia step 0, character n-grams, token count, Wikipedia popularity and GloVe.
- **Pipeline.** Everything is preregistered ([preregistration](preregistration.md)), with timestamped deviations. Stages run from simple to complex: (1a) top-3 PCA plots, (1b) confirmatory tests, (2) geometry and a Goodfire-style flat-subspace probe, (3) behavior and causal ablation/interchange, (4) ablations, (5) other models, (6) sensitivity.

**Interpretation.**
- **Positive.** Qwen3.5-2B encodes a modest but robust amount of within-group phylogenetic order that untrained networks and simple name statistics do not explain. A 10-dimensional linear subspace carries tree distances that generalize to held-out families.
- **Negative 1: lexical and co-occurrence statistics are not ruled out.** The order is not significant where spelling or co-occurrence disagree with the tree.
- **Negative 2: no time signal.** It does not follow divergence times beyond topology.
- **Negative 3: ecology wins on conflicts.** Where ecology and phylogeny conflict (whale vs hippo and shark), the geometry follows ecology. The dominant late-layer direction is an aquatic-to-terrestrial habitat axis, confirmed on held-out species.
- **Overall.** The picture is a representation that mixes folk taxonomy, ecology and some genuine phylogenetic signal. It is not a clean "tree of life" manifold like the one reported for Evo 2.

**Main limitations.**
- One main model of 2B parameters.
- Vernacular names only: Latin binomials carry no structure.
- The co-occurrence control uses GloVe, not the model's own training corpus.
- Topology comes from Open Tree and ranks from NCBI. Both are imperfect references for deep splits.
