# Literature review of phylogenetic structure in representations

Libor “Burny” Burian · revised 26 November 2026.

The scientific question is whether distances between animal **names** reflect topology inside broad taxonomic groups. A geometry measured for genomes, masked descriptions, lexical categories or geographic locations supplies a comparison or method. Those experiments use different inputs, targets and opportunities for shortcuts.

| Primary source | What was measured | Relevance to this study |
|---|---|---|
| [Goodfire, Finding the Tree of Life in Evo 2](https://www.goodfire.com/research/phylogeny-manifold) | Bacterial genome windows, pooled across windows and compared with GTDB branch-length distances. The authors report angular graph geodesics, a supervised angular subspace with clade holdouts and associations with nucleotide composition. | Motivates a graph and a probe comparison. English names and edge counts on a sampled Open Tree topology are different measurements. Goodfire's fitted correlations do not calibrate expected text-model accuracy. |
| [Cutinha, PhyloText Mammals](https://github.com/jadencutinha/phylotext-mammals) | Student repository comparing embeddings of masked mammal descriptions with a mammalian reference. | Directly relevant work using natural-language descriptions. A negative result there does not determine the outcome of a hidden-state name test. |
| [García Durán, organism representations](https://github.com/alexgarciaduran/llm_internal_representations) | An organism-oriented representation project with code and exploratory analyses. | A reason to avoid a universal priority claim. Our contribution is the particular within-group endpoint, controls and versioned panel. |
| [Park et al., The Geometry of Categorical and Hierarchical Concepts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01506) | A formalization of hierarchical concepts tested using WordNet categories in Gemma and LLaMA-3. | A lexical hierarchy and an evolutionary tree are distinct reference structures. |
| [Modell et al., The Origins of Representation Manifolds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18235) | Studies how conditional distributions of contexts relate to the geometry of learned representations. | Motivates distributional control comparisons. A single GloVe baseline cannot exclude all co-occurrence explanations. |
| [Engels et al., Not All Language Model Features Are One-Dimensionally Linear](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14860) | Identifies multidimensional representations, including circular representations of calendar concepts. | Calendar plots are descriptive checks of the plotting pipeline. They do not validate tree recovery. |
| [Gurnee and Tegmark, Language Models Represent Space and Time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02207) | Examines recoverability of spatial and temporal information from hidden states for named entities. | Motivates entity-name readouts and out-of-sample testing. Its prediction targets differ from phylogenetic relationships. |
| [Hewitt and Liang, Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/) and [Hewitt and Manning, A Structural Probe](https://aclanthology.org/N19-1419/) | Probe selectivity controls and supervised recovery of tree structure in linguistic representations. | Motivates shuffled targets and comparisons with raw features. Neither high in-sample fit nor global correlation demonstrates a dedicated mechanism. |
| [Geiger et al., Causal Abstractions of Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02997) | Tests correspondences between interventions in a proposed high-level account and a network. | Specifies why the edited quantity and measured change must match. A donor prediction after an edit can already have been present before it. |

The primary comparison uses publicly documented models and reference resources. [Qwen checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B/tree/15852e8c16360a2fea060d615a32b45270f8a8fc) · [Pythia suite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.01373) · [Open Tree](https://github.com/OpenTreeOfLife/germinator/wiki/Synthetic-tree-API-v3) · [TimeTree 5](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msac174)

The earlier review includes the broader search and figure transcriptions as a dated research record. This revision limits present-tense claims to the sources directly compared with this experiment.
