# Independent reanalysis protocol, 26 November 2026

Libor “Burny” Burian. This is a retrospective audit of revision `cc36b91`. The paper and its headline numbers were already public. The following decisions were fixed before this audit's statistical replay. This document does not amend the original registration.

1. Verify SHA-256 for each registered CSV/Newick pair and the activation row names. Rebuild the tree, distances, and decision-node means directly from the frozen files. Use the sampling order, random streams, layer selection and blocks specified by the run, including independent random streams per component. Reproduce the as-run shared-minimum rule first. For the written rule award one-third on any tied minimum, one for a uniquely correct pair and zero for a uniquely wrong pair. Keep the lexical and GloVe selection rules explicit and independent of tie credit. Restrict lexical-discordant sensitivity sets to a uniquely selected wrong pair.
2. For H1 require within-stratum max-T p < 0.05, a family-block lower 95% bound above one-third, and a positive lower bound against every compulsory control. Report the two-fold family cross-fit estimate and conditional interval at the modal layer. Repeat the paired comparison with orders as blocks. Check the original fixed eligibility mask and a sensitivity permutation that carries lexical head labels with each representation. Neither the finite panel nor random seeds measure variation across independently trained networks.
3. For S1 use the global Spearman permutation maximum over layers. For S2 show both the broad within-order reading and a narrower rank-resolved reading in which the sister pair shares a recorded family or genus that the outgroup does not. Recheck S3 and S4 with strict discordance, separately recording GloVe coverage. For S5 use the original pooled within-rank Spearman and the amended partial statistic controlling MRCA depth. For the amended family use a two-sided maximum absolute partial correlation over all layers and the same within-stratum species permutation at every layer. Report the fixed-layer result separately. Use 1,000 permutations and Holm across all five secondaries. Evaluate ties with averaged ranks. Shared phylogeny and the conditioning assumptions limit biological inference.
4. For causal work, look for saved aligned outcomes, reconstruct both the original and expanded random-subspace comparisons, and distinguish post-edit donor predictions from newly acquired donor predictions. If pairing is missing, publish sharp bounds and leave the literal C-swap comparison unevaluable. Preserve both time-ordered runs. For habitat, Holm-adjust over four registered slots with p=1 as a bookkeeping placeholder for an unevaluable hypothesis. Report held-out overlap, trait provenance and order-conditioned or order-level sensitivity where identifiable.
5. Identify which exploratory analyses have raw inputs, which have aggregate summaries, and which were deferred. Preserve source observations. Record denominators, nulls, missing units, multiplicity, scientific references and evidence level for each current conclusion. No LLM, probe, or other predictive model will be instantiated, fitted or executed. All visualizations and statistics use archived observations.

Method references include [rooted triples](https://doi.org/10.1093/sysbio/45.3.323), [random permutations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6405018/), [cross-validation and selection](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2303), [comparative-method dependence](https://doi.org/10.1086/284325), [Holm](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4615733), [equivalence testing](https://doi.org/10.1177/1948550617697177), and [preregistration](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1708274114).
