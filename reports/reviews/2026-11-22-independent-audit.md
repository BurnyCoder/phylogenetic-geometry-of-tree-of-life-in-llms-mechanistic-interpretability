# Independent audit of results, methods and claims (2026-11-22)

> Historical review. The [26 November reanalysis](2026-11-26-scientific-audit.md) independently rechecks the original observations and supersedes interpretations of the switch endpoint, secondary tests, tied choices and test families in earlier records.

*Libor "Burny" Burian's project; audit performed by Claude Code on 2026-11-22.*

## Scope and method
- **Independent.** Earlier reviews in `reports/reviews/` were not used as a starting point. Every claim in README, paper, summary and experiment notes was traced to code and results files, and the important ones were recomputed.
- **No new model inference or training.** Everything below is computed from stored activations (`DATA_DIR/activations`) and `results/**/*.json`. The re-derivations are a pipeline phase:
  ```powershell
  uv run python -m phylogeo.run --config configs/qwen35_2b.yaml --phases audit
  ```
  The phase is [`src/phylogeo/audit.py`](../../src/phylogeo/audit.py), tested in `tests/test_audit.py`. It writes `results/qwen35_2b/audit/results.json`, rendered in `results/RESULTS.md` and `results/qwen35_2b/SUMMARY.md`, section "Independent audit".
- **Literature.** Every external citation was re-checked on the live source: link, authors, title, year, and whether the source says what it is cited for.
- **Numbers below** are from `results/qwen35_2b/{stage1b,stage2,stage3,eco,audit}/*.json` as of this audit, layer 6 unless stated.

## Verdict
The registered pipeline is implemented correctly, and the headline numbers reproduce with independent code. No error invalidates the registered verdicts: H1 supported; S1, S2 supported; S3, S4, S5 not; C-swap supported; C-abl not; Addendum A aquatic end supported. The audit found five substantive problems in how results were described, and one fragility in the primary claim. All are fixed or disclosed in README, paper, summary, figures and experiment notes.

## What was recomputed and found correct
- **Primary metric (independent code).** All 200,192 resolved within-stratum, head-noun-disjoint triplets were enumerated over 174 decision nodes, with no per-node cap. LCAs came from dendropy parent pointers, not `RefTree`, and accuracy was node-balanced. Results: Qwen 0.474 (reported 0.475 with K = 200 sampling), random-init Qwen 0.381 (0.382), Pythia step 0 0.397 (0.402). The whole layer profile matches; the peak is at layer 6. `tests/test_audit.py` shows the enumerator equals the main sampler exactly when the cap is not reached.
- **Max-T test.** An independent 200-permutation rerun gives Qwen p at its floor, 1/201. The null 95th percentile of the maximum over layers is 0.390, and the observed value is 0.475.
- **Secondary family.** The Holm adjustment over S1–S5 was re-derived: S3, S4 and S5 all equal 0.360, because Holm is monotone (3 × 0.120).
- **Conflict triplets.** Binomial 11/36, two-sided p = 0.0288. Behaviour: 170/300, p = 0.0242. The expected agreement if independent, 0.567·0.613 + 0.433·0.387 = 0.515, matches.
- **Causal tests.**
  - The rank p-values were recomputed from the stored random-subspace effects. C-abl: 11 of 99 random drops are at least the probe's 0.083 (largest 0.106), so p = 0.12. C-swap: 0 of 99 reach 0.055, so p = 0.01. Holm gives 0.02 and 0.12. McNemar 3 vs 4, one-sided p = 0.773.
  - *Variance matching on the held-out species*, where the interventions act. The random subspaces were redrawn exactly (same fold and seed). They hold 12.6% of training-species variance but only 7.4% (6.0–8.9%) of held-out variance. The probe's held-out share is bounded above by 7.6%, from its stored all-species (9.96%) and training (12.3%) shares. The final run is therefore roughly matched where it matters, and the experiment note's "the first run under-matched its random controls" is supported.
- **Addendum A.** M1 is an exact Mann–Whitney p (U = 4; 6 marine vs 61 ground mammals). Counting arrangements gives 12 / C(67, 6) = 1.2e-7, equal to the reported value. Holm used the 3 evaluable tests instead of the registered 4, because there are no bats; with 4 the conclusions are unchanged.
- **Neighbour joining.** 70 of the reference tree's 388 non-trivial splits are recovered (18.0%), so "about a fifth" holds. The reference has polytomies, so this is not exactly 1 − nRF.
- **Lexically neutral subset.** 6,287 of its 6,306 triplets share no character 3-gram at all, 14 tie with the correct pair among the tied minima, and 5 tie the two wrong pairs. "Spelling has no preference" is accurate.
- **Gromov δ.** `treeness.delta_base_point` is the base-point formulation of Khrulkov et al. 2020 (δ_w = max(G ⊗_maxmin G − G), δ_rel = 2δ/diam), the same as their reference implementation. The brute-force four-point test in `tests/` checks it.
- **Pythia-1.4B steps 0 and 1** have identical stored activations (max difference 0), hence identical stage-5 numbers. Their checkpoint files differ on the Hub. Plausibly the first warm-up update is below bfloat16 resolution, and the models are loaded in bfloat16; this was not verified at the weight level.

## Are the methods used as the fields use them?
- **Rooted-triplet accuracy** follows the triplet idea of tree comparison ([Critchlow, Pearl & Qian 1996](https://doi.org/10.1093/sysbio/45.3.323)): topology only, invariant to monotone transforms of distance. Node balancing is this project's design choice, preregistered. A class-only geometry scores exactly 1/3 within strata (class oracle 0.333), as intended.
- **Max-T over layers** (Westfall & Young 1993, *Resampling-Based Multiple Testing*, Wiley) with a within-stratum permutation is a valid family-wise test for "TA_within exceeds what exchangeable within-stratum labels give". On S3/S4 the permutation reassigns species to triplet positions selected by the *true* names' spelling or GloVe, so the null no longer conditions on lexical discordance. Against a purely lexical model this is conservative; S3/S4 fail anyway.
- **Mantel tests** with Spearman and matrix permutation ([Mantel 1967](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6018555/)) are standard. Their weakness under phylogenetic autocorrelation ([Guillot & Rousset 2013](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.12018)) is acknowledged in the code, and S1 is correctly labelled "not specific to phylogeny".
- **Holm and BH-FDR.** BH uses `scipy.stats.false_discovery_control` over the 178 registered exploratory tests that have p-values. The intersection-union logic of H1 criterion 3 (all six paired CIs must exclude 0) needs no multiplicity correction.
- **Clustered (block) bootstrap.** Resampling families is the registered choice. The general advice for clustered data is to cluster at the coarsest level at which dependence is plausible, provided there are enough clusters ([Cameron & Miller 2015](https://doi.org/10.3368/jhr.50.2.317)). Taxa within an order share ancestry, and most within-stratum decision nodes in mammals and birds split orders. The order level (118 clusters) is therefore a defensible sensitivity analysis, and it is where A1 appears. The registered verdict stands, but "beyond untrained networks" should be read with this caveat. A follow-up should preregister order-level or hierarchical resampling.
- **Partial Spearman (S5)** is a standard partial correlation on ranks. Within rank bins, age and topological depth correlate at |ρ| up to 0.999, which leaves little independent variation; this is why S5 has low power.
- **Flat probe.** It follows Goodfire's published recipe. Selectivity uses a shuffled-tree control, in the spirit of control tasks ([Hewitt & Liang 2019](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/)). PCA is fit inside each fold. One small transductive step remains: species vectors are centred per template over all species, including held-out ones. This is negligible next to the class-only finding.
- **Interventions.** Mean ablation and interchange at the name tokens with forward hooks, against variance-matched random subspaces ([Geiger et al. 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02997)), are standard activation-patching practice. The probe subspace is learned on template-averaged, centred vectors and applied in a different prompt, the class question. This is a reasonable transfer, but it is untested.

## Remaining limitations (not fixable without new runs)
- Co-occurrence is the main open alternative. S4 uses GloVe (Wikipedia + Gigaword), not the model's own corpus, and there is no co-occurrence-neutral subset analogous to the spelling-neutral one.
- The positive within-group evidence beyond the untrained networks rests on mammals and birds. Its CI depends on the bootstrap block level (A1).
- The post-hoc lexically neutral result (A3) is the cleanest evidence that the trained model carries non-spelling information. It is post hoc and should be preregistered in any replication.
